## 1. About Privacy Lens

Privacy Lens is an Android privacy utility designed to help users understand the permissions associated with applications on their device.

Privacy Lens may display information including:

-   Applications installed on or visible from your Android device
    
-   Application names
    
-   Application icons
    
-   Application package names
    
-   Application versions
    
-   Permissions requested by applications
    
-   Permissions granted to applications
    
-   Android permission categories
    
-   Applications with access to particular permission categories
    
-   Permission-related activity when reliable information is made available by Android
    

Privacy Lens is intended to make Android permission information easier to understand.

----------

## 2. Privacy Lens Does Not Access Your Personal Content

Privacy Lens is designed to analyze **permission-related information**, not your personal content.

Privacy Lens does not intentionally access, read, collect, copy, upload, or share the contents of your:

-   Photos
    
-   Videos
    
-   Photo gallery
    
-   Personal files
    
-   Documents
    
-   Contacts
    
-   SMS messages
    
-   Call history
    
-   Calendar events
    
-   Camera
    
-   Microphone
    
-   Physical location
    

Privacy Lens may display whether another application has been granted permission to access one of these categories.

For example:

**Camera — Allowed**

means Android reports that the selected application has relevant camera permission.

It does not mean Privacy Lens accesses your camera.

----------

## 3. Permission Information

Privacy Lens obtains application and permission-related metadata using Android system APIs.

The Application may process information such as:

-   Application metadata
    
-   Package information
    
-   Permission declarations
    
-   Permission grant status
    
-   Permission categories
    
-   Android permission capabilities
    

This information is used to provide Privacy Lens functionality.

Privacy Lens is not designed to transmit your installed-app list or permission information to Privacy Lens servers or third parties.

Permission-related information used by the Application is processed locally on your device under the current design.

For more information, please review the Privacy Lens Privacy Policy.

----------

## 4. Local Storage

Privacy Lens may store application and permission-related information locally on your device.

A local SQLite database may be used to store information such as:

-   Application metadata
    
-   Package names
    
-   Permission names
    
-   Permission statuses
    
-   Permission categories
    
-   Synchronization timestamps
    
-   Permission capability information
    
-   Reliable permission activity information when available
    

Application icons may also be cached locally.

This local storage is used to improve performance and avoid repeatedly querying Android for the same information.

----------

## 5. Data Refresh

Privacy Lens may periodically refresh locally stored application and permission information.

Cached information is generally considered eligible for refresh after approximately three hours.

You may also manually refresh the information.

Android controls application lifecycle and background execution. Therefore, Privacy Lens does not guarantee that automatic synchronization will occur at an exact time or interval.

----------

## 6. Permission Granted vs. Permission Used

Privacy Lens distinguishes between an application being **granted a permission** and an application **actually using that permission**.

Having permission does not necessarily mean an application has recently used it.

For example:

**Microphone — Allowed**

means an application has relevant permission to access the microphone.

It does not necessarily mean the application is currently recording or recently recorded audio.

Privacy Lens will only display permission usage information when reliable information is available through supported Android APIs.

When reliable information is unavailable, Privacy Lens may display:

**Last access unavailable**

Privacy Lens does not intentionally fabricate or guess permission usage timestamps.
