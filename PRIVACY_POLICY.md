
# Privacy Policy — Privacy Lens

**Effective Date:** July 24, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 24, 2026

Privacy Lens is designed with privacy as its core principle.

Privacy Lens helps you understand which permissions applications installed on your Android device have been granted.

**Privacy Lens does not collect your personal information, does not access your personal files or content, and does not share your data with third parties.**

All permission analysis performed by Privacy Lens is designed to remain locally on your device.

----------

## 1. What Privacy Lens Does

Privacy Lens provides a privacy dashboard showing permission-related information about applications on your Android device.

The application may display information such as:

-   Application name
    
-   Application icon
    
-   Application package name
    
-   Application version
    
-   Permissions requested by an application
    
-   Permissions currently granted to an application
    
-   Permission categories supported by the device
    
-   Applications that have access to a particular permission
    
-   Permission-related usage information when Android reliably makes such information available
    

Privacy Lens uses Android system APIs to obtain this permission-related metadata.

This information is used only to provide Privacy Lens functionality.

----------

## 2. We Do Not Collect Personal Information

Privacy Lens does **not collect personal information about you**.

Privacy Lens does not require you to create an account.

We do not collect:

-   Your name
    
-   Email address
    
-   Phone number
    
-   Home or mailing address
    
-   Account credentials
    
-   Passwords
    
-   Advertising identifiers
    
-   Payment information
    
-   Personal profile information
    

Privacy Lens does not create a user profile or use your information for advertising.

----------

## 3. We Do Not Access Your Photos or Videos

Privacy Lens does **not access, read, upload, analyze, copy, or share your photos or videos**.

Privacy Lens does not browse your photo gallery.

The application may display whether another application has been granted a permission related to photos or videos.

For example:

> Photos and Videos — 5 apps allowed

This means Privacy Lens has identified permission-related metadata indicating that those applications have relevant access.

It does **not** mean Privacy Lens itself has accessed your photos or videos.

----------

## 4. We Do Not Access Your Files or Storage Content

Privacy Lens does **not browse, read, upload, copy, or share the personal files stored on your device**.

Privacy Lens does not inspect documents or other user-created files.

The application may display permission information indicating whether another application has access to files, media, or storage.

Privacy Lens reads the **permission status**, not the contents protected by that permission.

----------

## 5. We Do Not Access Your Contacts

Privacy Lens does not read, copy, upload, or share your contact list.

Privacy Lens may determine that another application has been granted a Contacts-related permission.

For example:

> Contacts — 7 apps allowed

Privacy Lens only uses permission-related metadata to provide this information.

It does not read the contacts themselves.

----------

## 6. We Do Not Access Your Camera or Microphone

Privacy Lens does not record audio using your microphone.

Privacy Lens does not capture photos or videos using your camera.

Privacy Lens may display whether another application has permission to use:

-   Camera
    
-   Microphone
    

Privacy Lens reads the permission information provided by Android.

It does not need to activate your camera or microphone to provide this functionality.

----------

## 7. We Do Not Track Your Location

Privacy Lens does not collect or track your physical location for its core functionality.

The application may display which applications have been granted Android location permissions.

For example:

> Location — 10 apps allowed

Privacy Lens reads the permission status associated with those applications.

It does not need to retrieve your actual location to provide this information.

----------

## 8. We Do Not Read Messages, Calls, or Calendar Content

Privacy Lens does not read the contents of your:

-   SMS messages
    
-   Phone calls
    
-   Call history
    
-   Calendar events
    

Privacy Lens may display whether another application has been granted Android permissions associated with these categories.

The application analyzes permission metadata rather than the personal content protected by those permissions.

----------

## 9. Permission Information

The primary information processed by Privacy Lens is permission-related device metadata.

Depending on the Android version and device, this may include:

-   Installed or otherwise visible applications
    
-   Application package names
    
-   Application names
    
-   Application icons
    
-   Application versions
    
-   Permissions declared by applications
    
-   Permission grant status
    
-   Permission categories
    
-   Android permission capability information
    
-   Permission-related activity information when reliably exposed by Android
    

This information is used exclusively to provide the Privacy Lens privacy dashboard.

----------

## 10. Local Processing

Privacy Lens is designed to process permission information **locally on your Android device**.

Permission analysis is not sent to a Privacy Lens server.

Privacy Lens does not upload your installed application list or permission information to our servers.

There is no cloud-based user profile associated with the core Privacy Lens functionality.

----------

## 11. Local Database

Privacy Lens uses a local SQLite database on your device to improve performance.

The database may store:

-   Application metadata
    
-   Package names
    
-   Permission names
    
-   Permission status
    
-   Permission categories
    
-   Permission capability information
    
-   Synchronization timestamps
    
-   Reliable permission activity information when available
    

This database exists within Privacy Lens's local application storage.

It is used so Privacy Lens does not need to repeatedly scan Android permission information every time you open a screen.

----------

## 12. Application Icons

Privacy Lens may cache application icons locally on your device to improve application performance.

Application icons are not uploaded to Privacy Lens servers.

----------

## 13. Data Refresh

Privacy Lens periodically refreshes its locally stored permission information.

Cached information is normally refreshed when it becomes approximately three hours old.

You may also manually refresh the information at any time.

Because Android controls background application execution, refresh timing may vary.

----------

## 14. Permission Granted Does Not Mean Permission Used

Privacy Lens distinguishes between an application having permission and an application actually using that permission.

For example:

> Camera — Allowed

means the application has permission to access the camera.

It does not automatically mean the application recently used the camera.

Privacy Lens only displays permission usage information when Android provides reliable information through supported APIs.

If reliable information is unavailable, Privacy Lens may display:

> Last access unavailable

Privacy Lens does not create or guess permission usage timestamps.

----------

## 15. No Data Is Shared With Third Parties

**Privacy Lens does not share your personal information, installed-app information, or permission information with third parties as part of the functionality described in this policy.**

We do not sell this information.

We do not rent this information.

We do not provide this information to advertisers.

We do not provide this information to data brokers.

We do not use your permission information to create advertising profiles.

----------

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and applicable store disclosures must be updated before those practices apply.

----------

## 17. No User Account

Privacy Lens does not require an account for its core functionality.

There is no Privacy Lens cloud account containing your permission information.

----------

## 18. Data Retention

Permission-related information stored by Privacy Lens remains locally on your device.

Privacy Lens may automatically remove outdated information, including:

-   Applications that are no longer installed or visible
    
-   Obsolete permission records
    
-   Old permission activity records
    
-   Old cached application icons
    

Historical permission activity, when available, may be retained locally for a limited period.

Uninstalling Privacy Lens will normally cause Android to remove Privacy Lens's locally stored application data, subject to Android's own backup, restore, and device-specific behavior.

----------
